## Purpose

This document establishes a **premium, minimal knowledge record** of the Growa platform for intellectual property registration, investor due diligence, and internal governance.

It follows a **black-box architecture** methodology: subsystem boundaries, capabilities, and sanitized interface contracts are documented; proprietary algorithms, calibration coefficients, and implementation logic remain protected.
