## Vision

GreenhouseOS by **Growa** transforms greenhouse design from static CAD into a **living virtual twin** — every design decision immediately surfaces financial, agronomic, and engineering consequences.

---

## Four Value Pillars

<div class="card-grid">

### Energy & Economics
Real-time OPEX modelling, energy intensity (kWh/m²), and ROI scenarios for climate equipment investments.

### Agronomic Intelligence
Industry-standard microclimate indices — vapour pressure deficit, daily light integral, and crop stress signals — computed from design context.

### Engineering Outputs
Actionable sizing guidance: heating capacity, dehumidification demand, and ventilation exchange rates.

### Industrial Bridge
Export of climate-control setpoints compatible with leading greenhouse automation vendors.

</div>
